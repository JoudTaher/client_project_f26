Campus Partner Resources

UMSI's Engaged Learning Office connects students with real-world projects that support communities and organizations while helping students build professional skills.

AI Clinic Project Experience

Students work in small collaborative teams to help organizations identify and implement AI tools. Projects involve real information challenges and include guidance from information professionals.

Eligibility: U-M undergraduate and graduate students
When: Fall and winter semesters

Learn more about the AI Clinic Project Experience

Student Organization Engaged Learning Leaders

Through SO-ELL, students participate in semester-long information projects for community-based organizations while gaining experience in consulting, project management, leadership, and information fields.

Eligibility: U-M undergraduate and graduate students
When: Fall and winter semesters
